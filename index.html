    How this is worked out

    • Each cigarette is taken to cost about 20 minutes of life at typical inhalation, the average from a 2024 study in the journal Addiction (news coverage). Light and deep inhalation scale that by 0.8× and 1.25×, which is a rough adjustment of mine. Life lost is capped at 15 years because an average stops being reliable at very heavy use.
    • The projection assumes you smoked today's amount every day since you started and keep going. Real outcomes vary a lot with genes, diet, and other health factors.
    • Nicotine, tar and carbon monoxide per stick are typical machine-rated values for each type, not measurements of your pack. If your pack lists its own figures, pick "Other" and enter them.
    • This is an estimate to help you decide, not medical advice.

        Cheers for you

          Estimates, not medical advice. Intake figures use typical machine-tested yields per cigarette and your inhalation setting, so real intake varies. Life figures use a rule of thumb of about 20 minutes of life per cigarette, scaled by inhalation depth and capped, based on published population studies of smokers. Recovery timelines are commonly cited by public health agencies. Talk to a doctor before changing medication or starting exercise.

          Need help quitting? In India you can call the National Tobacco Quitline at 1800-11-2356 (please verify the number on the official site) or ask your doctor about nicotine replacement and counselling.
