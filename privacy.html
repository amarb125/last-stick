Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026. Draft: have it reviewed by a lawyer and fill in the contact details before public launch.

Who we are

Last Stick is a quit-smoking tool run by [owner name]. Contact: [contact email].

Data that stays on your device

The calculator, streak, achievements and lung care log are stored only in your browser (local storage). We do not receive your cigarette counts, spending, age, health figures or practice history. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Squads (optional)

If you join Squads we store on our server: a generated name, a one-way hash of your backup code, your points and the achievements they came from, your current streak start time and best streak length, whether you appear on the public board, the squads you joined, the preset cheers you sent and received, and the time you were last active. We do not collect your real name, email, phone number or location, and there is no free-text chat.

Other squad members, and everyone if you choose the public board, can see your generated name, points and streak length. You must confirm you are 18 or older to join.

Why we use it

Only to run the leaderboards and cheers. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

Where it is processed

Squads data is stored with Supabase in the Asia Pacific (Mumbai) region. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details.

Your choices

  • Hide yourself from the public board at any time in Squads.
  • Download or delete your Squads data from Squads → You, where those buttons are shown, or by contacting us.
  • Keep your backup code private. Anyone who has it can act as you in Squads.

Retention

Squads data is kept until you delete your profile. Empty squads are removed.

Children

Squads is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

We will update this page and its date if this policy changes.
